Scouts who have completed 7th or 8th grade can apply to be in our Aides-in-Training (AIT) unit. This is a competitive application process because there are only **12 openings**.

{{< callout type="warn" >}}
**Status — Summer 2026** — We are no longer accepting AIT applications, but you can still join us as a Cadette camper.
{{< /callout >}}

## Application deadline

The application deadline is **February 14th**.

## How registration works

All AIT applicants register in the Council system as Cadettes during the general registration process. The Ashgrove application is separate, and decides which Cadettes join the AIT unit instead of a regular Cadette unit.

## What AITs do

AITs follow a structured leadership curriculum across the ten days of camp — shadowing unit leaders, planning small group activities, learning camp logistics, and earning specific Girl Scout leadership badges along the way.

Successful AITs are well-positioned to apply as Camp Aides in subsequent summers.
