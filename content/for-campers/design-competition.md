Each year, our t-shirt and patch are designed by a Girl Scout in our service area. Create a design that captures the current camp theme.

{{< callout type="warn" >}}
**Deadline** — Entries should be mailed by **February 14, 2026**.
{{< /callout >}}

## Guidelines

- Use a small, round paper plate or a five-inch circle. The official 2026 Design Competition Form is available to print.
- Design must include the words **"Ashgrove Adventure 2026"** and depict the theme, **"High Seas / Deep Seas"**.
- Each entry should include the artist's name, address, phone number, and troop number on the back.
- No limit to the number of entries per person.
- Individual Girl Scouts may also participate.
- Please do not use copyrighted images.

## Where to submit

Drop off entries at Ashgrove Task Group, or email a high-resolution version of your art to [ashgrove.design.competition@example.org](mailto:ashgrove.design.competition@example.org).

## Winners

Winners are automatically guaranteed enrollment in Ashgrove. (They still need to apply through the GSCNC website.) Ashgrove Adventure's Task Group votes on entries in March.

*Note: this is a camper competition. While AITs and Camp Aides may submit designs, please no adult submissions.*
