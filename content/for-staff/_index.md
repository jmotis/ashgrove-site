{{< tab "Task Group" >}}
## Task Group

The Task Group meets monthly through the school year to plan and prepare the summer's program. Anyone who has volunteered in a previous year and any adult considering volunteering is welcome.

We design the theme arc, plan centers, finalize the budget, recruit visiting experts, and coordinate logistics with Camp Crowell. Most meetings run two hours and end in time for the drive home.
