## Adult volunteers

Adult applications open each November and are due by **February 14th** for full consideration. Most positions need one week of your summer plus a handful of planning meetings during the school year.

Apply online: [Ashgrove Adult Volunteer Application →](#)

## Camp Aides

Camp Aides must have completed 9th grade *or* completed 8th grade and successfully completed Ashgrove Adventure's AIT program. They must also submit a complete application by the application deadline.

{{< callout type="warn" >}}
**Status — Summer 2026** — We are no longer accepting Camp Aide applications. Please join us next year.
{{< /callout >}}

## Background checks

All adult volunteers must complete a Girl Scouts Nation's Capital background check before camp. This is administered through GSNC's volunteer portal.
