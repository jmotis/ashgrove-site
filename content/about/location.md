Camp Crowell is a Girl Scouts Nation's Capital property in Oakton, VA, tucked between Difficult Run and the Cross County Trail. The grounds include open meadows, wooded trails, a dining hall, multiple unit shelters, and a swimming pool used during all-camp swim time.

## Address

11700 Hunter Mill Road, Oakton, VA 22124

## Getting there

Most families carpool to camp; carpool sign-ups are organized by unit in late spring. Drop-off is at the main parking area between 8:30 and 9:00 AM.
