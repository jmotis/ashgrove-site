<p class="lede">Founded in 1976, Ashgrove has grown from a single unit to a ten-day program serving roughly 250 campers each summer.</p>

## The first summer

Ashgrove was founded in the summer of 1976 by a handful of Girl Scout leaders who wanted a camp run by Scouts, for Scouts. The first session ran for a single week with three units and twenty-eight campers.

## Themes through the years

Every Ashgrove summer carries a theme that ties together songs, badges, costumes, and activities. Past themes include "Around the World in 10 Days," "Storybook Summer," and 2026's "High Seas, Deep Seas."

## 50th anniversary

Summer 2026 will mark Ashgrove's 50th season. We're planning an all-camp reunion at the end of camp — open to former campers, leaders, and volunteers across all five decades.
