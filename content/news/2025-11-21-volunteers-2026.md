Adult, Camp Aide, and AIT applications for Summer 2026 are due **February 14th**. Returning staff in good standing may apply through May.

The application is online this year — same questions, friendlier form. If you applied last year, log in with the same email and your prior answers will pre-fill.

## Who can apply

- **Adult volunteers** — unit leaders, lifeguards, kitchen, health staff. Background check required.
- **Camp Aides (CITs)** — high school Scouts in good standing.
- **AITs** — current 8th-graders entering the leadership track.
