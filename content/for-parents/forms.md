Every camper needs the following on file before the first day of camp:

- **Health form** — signed by a parent and (where applicable) a physician.
- **Medication form** — required for any prescription or OTC medication.
- **Pickup authorization** — list of adults authorized to pick up your camper.
- **Photo release** — optional; controls whether your camper appears in our published photos.

All forms are mailed in the welcome packet and can also be downloaded from our [forms page](#).
