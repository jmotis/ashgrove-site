{{< tab "Transportation" >}}
## How do campers get to and from camp?

Most families carpool or drop campers off directly at Camp Crowell. We do not provide bus service. Carpool sign-ups open in late spring and are organized by unit.

Drop-off is between 8:30 and 9:00 AM at the main parking area. Pickup is from 3:30 to 4:00 PM at the same location. Please be on time — staff cannot remain after 4:15.

An adult must sign campers in and out each day. Anyone other than a parent picking up must be listed on the camper's pickup authorization form.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab "Medication" >}}
## How is medication handled at camp?

All medications — prescription and over-the-counter — must be checked in with the Camp Nurse on the first day of camp, in their original containers. Nothing should be in a camper's backpack.

Our Camp Nurse is on site for the full camp day and administers all medication per the directions on the medication form, which must be signed by a parent or guardian.

If your camper carries an inhaler or epi-pen, it can stay with their unit leader. Please flag this on the health form so we can plan accordingly.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab "Camp Attire" >}}
## What should my child wear to camp?

Ashgrove takes place in an outdoor, summer setting. Every day, campers should bring a cup, sit-upon, poncho, and a lunch that does not require refrigeration. **Please write your child's name on all items.**

Due to hiking trails, ticks, poison ivy, and potential sunburns, appropriate camp attire is:

{{< checks >}}
- Hat or other head covering
- Shoulders covered (no tanks)
- No bare midriff
- No short shorts or skirts
- Ankles & shins covered
- Bandanna for hand drying
- Tie-on or velcro shoes
- Sunscreen + bug spray (applied at home)
{{< /checks >}}

{{< callout type="warn" >}}
Under no circumstances should a camper bring **cell phones, mp3 players, or umbrellas**. Due to camp health regulations, all bug spray and suntan lotion must be applied at home and not at camp.
{{< /callout >}}

{{< tab "Contacting Camp" >}}
## How do I reach my child at camp?

For routine messages, please call the Camp Director's line — posted in your welcome packet — and we'll relay a message at the next break. For emergencies, call the same number; it's monitored throughout the camp day.

Campers should not bring cell phones. Should an emergency arise on the camper's end (illness, injury), staff will contact you directly using the emergency contact information on file.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab "Inclement Weather" >}}
## What happens in bad weather?

Camp runs rain or shine. Campers should come prepared with a poncho and weather-appropriate layers. In the event of thunderstorms, units shelter in the dining hall and adjacent buildings until the storm passes.

In the rare event camp is canceled, families will be notified by 7:00 AM via the camp email list and our Facebook page. Refunds for canceled days are issued on a prorated basis.
