Ashgrove is run entirely by volunteers, and the easiest path in is through your camper's unit. Most volunteer roles need just one week of your summer, plus a handful of planning meetings during the school year.

Common roles:

- **Unit leader** — runs one unit alongside another adult
- **Lifeguard** — required certification, runs swim time
- **Kitchen** — preps and serves lunches; runs all-camp meals
- **Health staff** — RN, EMT, or comparable certification

See the [For Staff section](/for-staff/) for application details.
