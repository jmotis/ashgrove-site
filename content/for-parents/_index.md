Welcome, parents. Ashgrove is a ten-day Girl Scout day camp at Camp Crowell. Your camper will spend her days with a unit of about 24 campers and 4 leaders, rotating through centers, songs, badges, and outdoor activities.

The pages in this section answer the most common questions. If yours isn't here, please reach out — we usually reply within a day.
