title: "Camper Fees"
weight: 30
intro: "What it costs to send a camper to Ashgrove, and what's included."
---

The full ten-day Ashgrove program costs **$260–$300** per camper, depending on grade level and timing of registration. Fees include:

- The camp tee and patch (year-specific)
- All program materials and craft supplies
- All-camp meals (lunch each day; overnight dinner and breakfast for older units)
- Swimming, archery, and badge fees

## Financial assistance

We never want cost to be the barrier to a Girl Scout attending Ashgrove. Financial assistance is available through Girl Scouts Nation's Capital — apply during the regular registration process.
